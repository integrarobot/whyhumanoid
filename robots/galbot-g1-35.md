## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: 173
- **Weight (kg)**: 92.5
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: 5
- **Runtime (hours)**: 8
- **Walking speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Running speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Unveiled**: 2025
- **Platform and mobility**: Wheeled humanoid on 4-wheel omnidirectional chassis (600×600 mm); 650 mm leg-lift travel; max 1.5 m/s; IP54
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 21 excl. chassis and end effectors: neck 2, arms 7×2, waist 3, legs 2; swappable dexterous hand / suction cup / gripper
- **Sensors and compute**: Head binocular camera; 4 torso RGB cameras + IMU; 2 wrist depth cameras + 2 wrist 6-axis F/T sensors; chassis 3D LiDAR, 8 ultrasonic sensors, IMU; NVIDIA AGX Orin 64 GB (275 TOPS); 2× 4-mic arrays; 5.5" touchscreen
- **Battery and charging**: 48 V 30 Ah battery; 8 h working time; 3.5 h adapter charge (54.6 V 10 A)
- **Software**: AI-driven control; multimodal perception; voice recognition
