## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: 140 (four-wheel mode) / 175 (two-wheel stance)

- **Running speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Unveiled**: 2024-12-26 (unveiled, China Robotics Network annual conference)
- **Platform and mobility**: Full-size wheel-legged humanoid; variable 4-wheel / 2-wheel mobility, climbs stairs
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 38 (11 per dexterous hand)
- **Sensors and compute**: LiDAR, cameras, millimeter-wave radar, microphones; 5G/Wi-Fi
- **Battery and charging**: GAC all-solid-state battery; 6 h
- **Software**: GAC pure-vision autonomous-driving algorithms; FIGS-SLAM; cloud multimodal LLM; 3D-GS + VR teleoperation
- **Voice**: Yes; responds to complex voice commands (cloud multimodal model)
- **Clothed**: No
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Autonomous navigation (indoor/outdoor SLAM, cm-level), obstacle avoidance; VR teleoperation for data collection
