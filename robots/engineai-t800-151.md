## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: 173
- **Weight (kg)**: 75-85 (varies by configuration)
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: 5 per dexterous hand (Pro/Max)
- **Runtime (hours)**: 3-5
- **Walking speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Running speed (km/h)**: ≥7.2 (≥2 m/s, hardware supported)
- **Unveiled**: 2025-12-02 (T800 unveiling, company)
- **Platform and mobility**: Full-size bipedal humanoid; aviation aluminium + titanium; active leg-joint cooling
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 25 (Basic); 29 + 14 hand = 43 (Pro); 32 + 14 = 46 (Max); 7-DoF hands with tactile sensing
- **Sensors and compute**: Wide-FOV binocular head camera; 3D LiDAR (not on Basic); 8-core CPU
- **Battery and charging**: Modular solid-state lithium battery, quick-release; up to 4 h claimed in marketing, 3-5 h in spec table
- **Software**: Orin NX 16G (Basic) / AGX Orin 64G, upgradable to Thor (Open Source, Pro, Max); secondary development except Basic
- **Voice**: Microphone and speaker
- **Clothed**: No
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Martial-arts demos; autonomy level not quantified
