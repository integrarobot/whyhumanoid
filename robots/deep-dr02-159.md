## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: 180
- **Weight (kg)**: ~68 (DR02); ~78 (DR02 Pro)
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: 10 continuous
- **Runtime (hours)**: ~3 unloaded; ~2.5 loaded
- **Walking speed (km/h)**: 3.6 max operating (1 m/s); 5.4 walking speed stated in marketing (1.5 m/s)
- **Running speed (km/h)**: 14.4 top tested (4 m/s, lab)
- **Unveiled**: Not verified
- **Platform and mobility**: Industrial all-weather bipedal humanoid; IP66; -20 to 55 °C; stairs 25 cm, slopes 20°
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 21 (DR02: 4 per arm, 6 per leg, 1 waist); 31 (Pro: 7 per arm, 3 waist, 2 head)
- **Sensors and compute**: Wide-angle camera; Pro adds LiDAR, 3 depth cameras and AI processor (NVIDIA AGX Orin 275 TOPS standard, AGX Thor optional)
- **Battery and charging**: ~2.5 h charge; 72 V external power interface
- **Software**: Not disclosed
- **Voice**: Speaker + microphone array
- **Clothed**: No
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Not quantified
