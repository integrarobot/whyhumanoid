## Before comparing

Research open-source build; fingertip reach floor to 2.23 m; parts cost excludes labor and integration

## Specifications

- **Unveiled**: 2026-11 (v1.0 open platform release; arXiv 2611.04083)
- **Platform and mobility**: Wheeled; Anthropomorphic mobile bimanual robot: two 7-DOF arms, pitching torso, 0.635 m lift, 3-DOF head, holonomic base
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 56 functional DOF (59 motors): 2×17-DOF ORCA hands, 2×7-DOF arms, 3-DOF head, 1-DOF torso pitch, lift
- **Sensors and compute**: Stereo head camera view streamed to Vision Pro; synchronized camera recording
- **Software**: Open-source stack: teleoperation, control, simulation and learning pipeline; Apple Vision Pro whole-body teleoperation
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Teleoperated demonstration collection and policy training (AI harness); not a turnkey autonomous product
