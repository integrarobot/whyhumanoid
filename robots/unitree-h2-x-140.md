- **Running speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed

- **Platform and mobility**: Full-size bipedal humanoid; positioned for security patrol, industrial, commercial and home services
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 31 total: 6 per leg, 7 per arm, 3 waist, 2 head; EDU optional dexterous hands
- **Sensors and compute**: Head wide-FOV binocular camera + chest wide-angle monocular camera (EDU optional chest RGB-D); 8-core CPU
- **Battery and charging**: Dual hot-swappable batteries, 20 Ah (1.152 kWh) total; magnetic body charging
- **Software**: OTA; EDU: user development on Intel Core i7 or Orin NX, optional Thor T5000 / Orin AGX
- **Voice**: Array microphone + high-power speaker; LED matrix head display
- **Clothed**: No
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Autonomy level not quantified (company)
