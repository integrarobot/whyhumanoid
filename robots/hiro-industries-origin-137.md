## Before comparing

Pre-delivery: the deposit buys a queue place, not a delivered unit. Kitting and light-assembly examples are labelled "Illustrative" by the company. Team of 2 (YC page). Location discrepancy: reserve page says Corvallis, Oregon; YC page says San Francisco. CEO Lex Roller was a founding engineer at Nori Robotics (row: Nori)

- **Unveiled**: Reservations open (deposit page live, accessed 2026-11-19). Estimated delivery Spring 2027, US addresses only: manufacturer estimate, not a confirmed date
- **Platform and mobility**: Stationary dual-arm platform for human-scale workstations, with a 1-DoF lift; no legs and no mobile base stated. Not a bipedal humanoid
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 17 DoF: 14 arm (7 joints per arm) + 2 gripper + 1 lift; parallel grippers
- **Sensors and compute**: 3 RGB-D cameras (head and wrist views); compute not disclosed
- **Battery and charging**: 1.54 kWh; estimated 2-4 h mixed use; about 1 h from 0 to 100% (manufacturer)
- **Software**: Teach / train / deploy workflow: record demonstrations, train a model, run it under supervision; teleoperation combined with AI (YC page). Model and software stack not named
- **Voice**: Not disclosed
- **Clothed**: Not disclosed
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Runs trained models under supervision and records new data; teleoperation plus AI; autonomy level not quantified (company)

## Market

- **Price and availability**: Manufacturer-stated total price USD 9,950 for the complete dual-arm robot. Reservation = USD 1,450 deposit, fully refundable any time before shipping (request by email); USD 8,500 balance due only when HIRO confirms the build is ready to ship. Estimated delivery Spring 2027, US addresses only; if it moves, buyer may cancel for a full refund (hiroindustries.com/reserve, accessed 2026-11-19). Taxes, shipping and warranty not stated
- **B2B clients**: Not disclosed; target tasks stated by the company: packing, kitting, machine tending and light assembly
