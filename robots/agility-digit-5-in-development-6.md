## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: 181
- **Weight (kg)**: 129
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: 22.7
- **Runtime (hours)**: 1.5
- **Walking speed (km/h)**: Not published for Digit 5
- **Running speed (km/h)**: Not published for Digit 5
- **Unveiled**: 2026-09-15 (unveiling)
- **Platform and mobility**: Industrial bipedal humanoid
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: Interchangeable tools; ISO flange mounting; DoF unpublished
- **Sensors and compute**: Cameras, depth and IMU; NVIDIA IGX Thor / Halos collaboration
- **Battery and charging**: 90 min use / 9 min charging announced; &gt;20 h/day includes recharging
- **Software**: Agility Arc; WMS/WES/MES integration
- **Voice**: Not disclosed
- **Clothed**: No
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Task autonomy; independent safety controller; Arc platform
