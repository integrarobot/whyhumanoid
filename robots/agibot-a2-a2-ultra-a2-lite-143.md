## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: 169
- **Weight (kg)**: ≈69 (Ultra); ≈64 (Lite)
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: ≈2 max per arm
- **Runtime (hours)**: Standing 3 (Ultra) / 4.5 (Lite); walking 1.5+
- **Walking speed (km/h)**: Up to 4.3 (1.2 m/s, Ultra); 2.9 (0.8 m/s, Lite)
- **Running speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Unveiled**: 2024-08-18 (A2 launch, AgiBot launch event)
- **Platform and mobility**: Full-size bipedal humanoid for reception, guidance and performances
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 40 (Ultra: 2 neck, 7 per arm, 6 per leg, 6-DoF dexterous hands); 23 (Lite)
- **Sensors and compute**: Ultra: 3D LiDAR, RGB-D, RGB and fisheye cameras; 16-core CPU + NVIDIA Jetson Orin 64G
- **Battery and charging**: 14.4 Ah; fast charging and swappable battery; 2 h charge
- **Software**: AimMaster client; LinkSoul (persona, knowledge base); swarm control; OTA every 2-3 months
- **Voice**: Array microphone + speaker; facial expression screen (Ultra)
- **Clothed**: Customizable shells/IP styling
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Autonomous navigation and obstacle avoidance; guided tours; facial-recognition greeting (company)
