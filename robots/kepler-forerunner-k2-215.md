## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: 175
- **Weight (kg)**: 75
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: 15 per arm; up to 30 dual-arm (company)
- **Runtime (hours)**: 8
- **Walking speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Running speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Unveiled**: 2025-05 (K2 "Bumblebee" debut at ICRA 2025, company release)
- **Platform and mobility**: Bipedal or wheeled; Bipedal humanoid (Basic / Bipedal Development versions); Wheeled Development version on intelligent wheeled chassis (135 kg)
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 52 (Development versions): 6 per leg, 7 per arm, 11-DoF tactile dexterous hand each; Basic version 30 DoF without dexterous hands
- **Sensors and compute**: Pure vision system, auditory sensor, flexible tactile sensors (25 contact points per finger), 6-axis F/T sensor per wrist, inertial sensors; 100 TOPS GPU (Development versions); &gt;80 integrated sensors (company release)
- **Battery and charging**: 1-hour charge; 8 h endurance (company)
- **Software**: Nebula system: simulation, modular motion/vision/voice functions, visual programming, multi-robot collaboration, online debugging, OTA; hierarchical end-to-end embodied AI
- **Voice**: Auditory sensor + speaker; voice functions in Nebula system
- **Clothed**: Not disclosed
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Hierarchical end-to-end embodied AI; executes task sequences autonomously in structured environments (company)
