## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: Not disclosed
- **Weight (kg)**: Not disclosed
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: Not disclosed
- **Runtime (hours)**: Not disclosed

- **Running speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Unveiled**: Company founded 2026-03-03; no product released. Poke-Aurora world-action model and RoboAPI technical preview: 2026-10-30. Founder targets first usable home robots in China ~2028
- **Platform and mobility**: Wheeled base with two arms, according to founder interview (QQ News, 2026-09-03); company rejects bipedal humanoid form.
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: Dual-arm manipulation focus; founder says no 21-DoF all-purpose design. DoF and end effectors not disclosed
- **Sensors and compute**: Not disclosed. In-house data capture: exoskeleton, UMI-style gloves (5-6 iterations) and first-person video
- **Battery and charging**: Not disclosed
- **Software**: Poke-Aurora world-action model (WAM: video in, actions out; not VLA); first-gen embodied world model 32B parameters (company, 2026-04); WAM x RL (real-robot reinforcement learning) x DATA; UAG parallel pre-training architecture (&gt;5x training efficiency claimed); RoboAPI non-commercial technical preview
- **Voice**: Natural-language instructions via RoboAPI (vision-language input); voice not disclosed
- **Clothed**: Not disclosed
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Company demos (2026-06): mapo tofu cooked end-to-end in ~9 min without human intervention; cloth folding; sachet tying; zip-tie threading. Poke-Aurora: 74.2% average success on ~10,000 unseen tasks with ~1,000 unseen objects, claimed +51.3% vs pi0.5 and +16.4% vs GPT-6 Astra (points vs relative not stated). Company-run evaluations, not independent
