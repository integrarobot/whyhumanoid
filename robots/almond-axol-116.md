- **Weight (kg)**: 19.5
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: 4 rated / 6.5 peak per arm
- **Platform and mobility**: Arms for table / cart / optional Jelly Mobile base
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: Two 7-DoF arms; 860 mm reach each
- **Battery and charging**: Optional Jelly Mobile: 2.4 kWh
- **Software**: Open Python SDK; CAD published; 500 Hz control
