## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: 173
- **Weight (kg)**: 57
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: 20.4 (2021 concept, 45 lb; not restated on Integrarobot dossier); 11 kg battery tray demonstrated (Oct 2024)
- **Runtime (hours)**: Not disclosed
- **Walking speed (km/h)**: 8 (max speed, 2021 design target)
- **Running speed (km/h)**: Not published
- **Unveiled**: 2023-12 (Gen 2 hardware reveal)
- **Platform and mobility**: Bipedal general-purpose humanoid; stairs and uneven terrain (Oct 2024); articulated toes and foot force/torque sensors; 30% faster walk vs prior gen (Dec 2023); obstacle/person avoidance via onboard nets; max speed ~8 km/h (2021 design target)
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: Neck 2 DoF (Dec 2023). Gen 2 hands (Dec 2023): 11 DoF, tactile sensing on all fingers. Later Optimus hand (Musk Jan 2025; patent Apr 2026): 22 DoF biomimetic, tendon-driven, actuators in the forearm, 25 actuators per arm (50 for both hands). Whole-body DoF not published.
- **Sensors and compute**: Eight cameras and the same Autopilot computer as Tesla vehicles (2021 announcement); joint position encoders; force/torque sensors in the feet; tactile sensing in all fingers; actuators with integrated electronics (Dec 2023); rear camera used to dock at the charger
- **Battery and charging**: Tesla-designed battery pack (AI Day 2022). Can locate the nearest charging station and dock autonomously using only the rear camera (Oct 2024). Capacity, voltage and runtime not published.
- **Software**: Tesla end-to-end neural nets on the vehicle Autopilot/FSD computer; onboard real-time inference; training from human demonstrations; multi-robot shared environment understanding
- **Voice**: Not disclosed
- **Clothed**: No
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Autonomous general-purpose humanoid (balance, navigation, perception). End-to-end onboard nets; autonomous error correction; learns tasks from human demonstrations; can fetch and hand over items on request. Tested in Tesla factories and offices (Nov 2025). Still in development; not a finished consumer product.

## Market

- **Price and availability**: No list price. Tesla/Musk targets: production cost &lt;$20,000 at &gt;1 million units/year (Jan 2025); long-term cost $20,000–$30,000 (Oct 2024). Selling price to be set by demand. Pilot line running at Fremont (Nov 2025). Internal Tesla use first; start of deliveries to outside companies estimated 2H 2026 (Jan 2025, rough). Gen 3 production line described as larger and due in 2026.
- **B2B clients**: Not disclosed
