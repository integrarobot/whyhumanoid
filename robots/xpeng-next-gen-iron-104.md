## Before comparing

First-gen IRON (2024) was 178 cm / 70 kg; XPENG has not published Next-Gen IRON height/weight (press citing 178/70 refers to gen 1). Some press quotes 2,250 TOPS vs official 3,000 TOPS effective

- **Platform and mobility**: Bipedal humanoid with humanoid spine, bionic muscles and full-body flexible skin; body-shape customization
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 82 DoF whole body; dexterous hands 22 DoF each; 4-DoF scapular shoulder
- **Sensors and compute**: 3 in-house Turing AI chips, 3,000 TOPS effective compute; tactile sensors embedded in skin (company)
- **Battery and charging**: All-solid-state battery; capacity and runtime not published
- **Software**: Physical-world large model "VLT + VLA + VLM" (company); SDK to be opened to developers
- **Voice**: Conversation via VLT + VLA + VLM; head-mounted 3D curved display integrating seeing, hearing, speaking and expression (company)
- **Clothed**: No
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Commercial service roles first (guided tours, shopping guides, traffic diversion); Baosteel industrial inspection partnership (company)
