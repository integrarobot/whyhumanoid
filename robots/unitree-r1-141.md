## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: 123
- **Weight (kg)**: ~29 (R1, R1 EDU); ~27 (R1 AIR)
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: ~3-5 per arm (manufacturer; varies with posture)
- **Runtime (hours)**: ~1
- **Walking speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Running speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Unveiled**: 2025-07-25 (R1 launch, Reuters)
- **Platform and mobility**: Lightweight bipedal humanoid
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 26 (R1): 6 per leg, 5 per arm, 2 waist, 2 head; 20 (R1 AIR); 26-40 (EDU, optional dexterous hand)
- **Sensors and compute**: Binocular camera (R1, EDU); monocular (AIR); 8-core CPU; EDU optional Orin (40-100 TOPS)
- **Battery and charging**: Quick-release lithium battery; ~1 h
- **Software**: Large multimodal model (voice and images); open joint/sensor interfaces; mainstream simulation platforms; secondary development only on EDU
- **Voice**: 4-mic array + speaker
- **Clothed**: No (custom stickers)
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Autonomy depends on development; ~1 h runtime
