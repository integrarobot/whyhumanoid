# CLOiD by LG

Catalogue snapshot: 2026-11-27. Catalogue entries report what their sources say. Check the original source, date, model and test conditions. Missing values are left blank or marked as unknown.

## Before comparing

LG's CES releases call it a home robot; LG lists it among its humanoid robots in the KIRIA release (2026-11-16). Do not carry over KAPEX specifications. LG does not say CLOiD uses AXIUM actuators.

## Specifications

- **Height (cm)**: 105-143 (adjustable via waist angle)
- **Weight (kg)**: Not disclosed
- **Payload or carry (kg)**: Not disclosed
- **Runtime (hours)**: Not disclosed
- **Walking speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Running speed (km/h)**: Not disclosed
- **Unveiled**: Unveiled at CES 2026 (Las Vegas, January 2026); no commercial launch date
- **Platform and mobility**: Head, torso with two arms, wheeled base with autonomous navigation; tilting torso adjusts height; no legs
- **Degrees of freedom and grippers**: 7 DoF per arm (shoulder 3, elbow 1, wrist 3); five individually actuated fingers per hand; arm length about 87 cm
- **Sensors and compute**: Head chipset; cameras and various sensors, not specified
- **Battery and charging**: Not disclosed
- **Software**: LG in-house VLM and VLA on the chipset, trained on tens of thousands of hours of household task data; Affectionate Intelligence; ThinQ / ThinQ ON
- **Voice**: Voice-based generative AI; speaker and display
- **Clothed**: Not disclosed
- **Autonomy and supervision**: Autonomous navigation; VLA plans and executes actions; CES household tasks were demonstrations, not deployments

## Market
