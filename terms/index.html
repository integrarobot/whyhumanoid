Terms of use

Terms of use for the Why Humanoid? MCP connection

These terms cover the public, read-only connection to Why Humanoid? through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). By connecting a client or an agent to it, you accept them. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

Who provides it

Why Humanoid? and this connection are provided by Integrarobot, founded by Javier Martín (see About). Questions about these terms: javier@integrarobot.com.

What the connection does

A remote MCP server at https://whyhumanoid-premium.javier-198.workers.dev/mcp with 6 read-only tools: list_categories, search_catalogue, get_rows, get_atlas, get_relations, account_status. They read the Why Humanoid? research catalogue of humanoid robots and their ecosystem, and its published atlases.

The tools only read. They do not control or send commands to any robot, do not create, change or delete records, and do not place orders, make payments or reach accounts, custom reports or internal tables.

Public access and the separate subscription

No account or key is needed. Without a key, the connection reads the public catalogue: the free categories in full, and Deployments and Components at their public fields.

The complete Deployments and Components are a separate paid subscription, described on Subscription and reports. Connecting without a key does not unlock them; they are read only with an API key of an active subscription, as explained on For agents.

What the data is, and its limits

Each record compiles claims made in public sources, with a link to the source, as the source states them. Records are not independently verified facts: manufacturer claims are shown as claims, not as verified capabilities, and a missing value stays missing. Records may be incomplete or out of date. How records are kept is explained in the Method.

The catalogue is research and data, not a personalised recommendation. Check the original sources before you rely on a record for a decision.

Sources

The rights in the source material stay with its owners. Using the connection does not transfer any rights in it. When you use a record, link the original source it cites.

Fair use

Requests made with an API key are limited to 60 a minute and 5,000 a day per key; over the limit, requests get HTTP 429 with a Retry-After header, and nothing is charged. Keep automated use of the public connection within the same limits. Do not try to reach data the connection does not offer, or to get around these limits or the access rules.

Availability and changes

There is no service level commitment. The connection, its tools and the catalogue may change, be interrupted or be withdrawn. These terms may change; the date above shows the last update.

Privacy

The public connection needs no account. How Why Humanoid? handles information is described on Privacy.
