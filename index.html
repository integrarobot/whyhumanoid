How to read this data.

The catalogue reproduces the Humanoids tab as it stands. What is unknown is flagged, never filled in.

Where does it come from?
From the Humanoids tab of the Integrarobot database. Each record links the sources consulted and the date of its last check, on 29 or 30 October 2026.
Not disclosed, not verified
Not disclosed means the manufacturer does not publish it. Not verified means there is a figure but it has not been cross-checked. Neither means zero or false.
Status of each record
Two marks per record. F: funding has been checked against a source or is still pending. S: specifications have been reviewed, carried over without revalidation, or are not published by the manufacturer.
Why are there no funding totals?
Rounds mix closed, announced and under-negotiation amounts, in different currencies and on different dates. Several rows explicitly warn against adding them up, so the catalogue adds none.
Are the figures comparable?
Not directly. Some are top speeds rather than working pace, instantaneous rather than sustained loads, or belong to different versions of the same robot. Each record includes a “Before comparing” note with the caveats for that model.
Why 133 records and not 135 rows?
Two rows are excluded because the sheet itself says so: a Genesis duplicate and the Integrarobot editorial entry.
Platform “no data”
Many records do not yet have the platform field filled in. The catalogue does not assume whether a robot walks, rolls or is a fixed torso.
Units
Height in centimetres, mass and payload in kilograms, runtime in hours and speed in km/h, as recorded in the sheet. Values are shown exactly as written there.
