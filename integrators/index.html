How to read this

  • Source. The Integrators tab of the Why Humanoid? master workbook: 28 providers, 26 fields each, reviewed 2026-11-24. Values appear as the sheet states them; nothing is filled in or recalculated. Snapshot 2026-11-25.
  • Claims, not verified results. Most records are provider or partner statements. Each profile shows its verification note and limitations next to the claims, with its sources.
  • Who counts. 24 providers show explicit humanoid work or a humanoid offering. That is not 24 validated production integrators: the evidence chart shows what kind of evidence each record has. CA3 (requested specialist exception, simulated tests only) and three historical records with unconfirmed humanoid scope are listed but never counted.
  • Evidence groups. Named project, corroborated: A named customer or partner project confirmed by the customer, a partner or a joint announcement. Pilot or proof-of-concept scale unless the sources say more. Named project, provider-reported: A named project or operated robot described by the provider itself, without customer or partner corroboration in the reviewed sources. Partnership announced: An integration or service partnership or agreement announced by the provider, the manufacturer or both. No named end-customer completion. Published technical or research work: Published documentation, code, lab engineering or a university-confirmed collaboration. Not a customer deployment. Commercial offering: A described humanoid product or service offering, sometimes with a demonstrator or a proposed protocol. No named completed humanoid project. Simulated tests only: Requested specialist exception: provider-reported simulated tests. No humanoid validation, no completed humanoid integration and no safety or unlearning guarantee. Humanoid scope unconfirmed: Historical record kept for continuity. Reviewed sources show service-robot work but do not establish humanoid work. Each provider’s group cites a phrase of its own Evidence stage cell.
  • Tags. Roles, services, platforms, sectors and service territory are read from the cells with fixed word lists. A term outside the lists is not tagged, and the full cell is always shown. Service territory comes only from the Service coverage cell, never from a headquarters or a project location.
  • Offered is not delivered. A service in the Services cell is an offer. A delivered project appears only under Named project and in the projects list, with its evidence stage.
  • Contact. Through each provider’s public website. Why Humanoid? publishes no personal contact details.
  • Free. The whole category, every profile and every source are free, with no account.
