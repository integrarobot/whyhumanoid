Advantages and drawbacks

As summarised in the research compilation, not an independent assessment.

Advantages
Extremely high spatial resolution; plug-and-play; commercial support; spare parts available
Drawbacks
Bulky for anthropomorphic fingers; only 25 fps; heats up at maximum rate (datasheet); no calibrated force
