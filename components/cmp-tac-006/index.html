What it senses

Sensing principle
Optical per pillar: LED + pinhole + photodiodes inside silicone pillars
Measured quantities
Per pillar: 3D displacement, 3D force and vibration. Global: 3D force, 3D torque, incipient slip, friction

9 pillars per array (3×3); kit with 2 arrays

Pitch 7.0 mm; pillars Ø6.0 mm

Drawbacks
