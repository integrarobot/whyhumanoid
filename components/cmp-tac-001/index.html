Advantages and drawbacks

As summarised in the research compilation, not an independent assessment.

Advantages
Very cheap and compact; open ecosystem (TACTO, PyTouch, datasets); swappable gel; purchasable
Drawbacks
Only the front face is sensitive; no force calibration; gel wears out; low frame rate compared with electrical sensors

Notes and uncertainties

Notes and uncertainties
The CC BY-NC license prevents commercial use of the design; sales are through GelSight.
