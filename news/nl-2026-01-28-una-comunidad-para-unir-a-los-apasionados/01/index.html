💰 ARK Big Ideas 2026 is the annual report of one of the investment funds most convinced that technology is the lever to activate in order to transform the world. And this year its report once again places robotics among the 12 technologies to invest in. Specifically, in this case they tell us we are at the beginning of the growth curve in which robotics will start to scale across all of industry, moving from an era of specialized robots to the arrival of AI-driven generalist robots.
