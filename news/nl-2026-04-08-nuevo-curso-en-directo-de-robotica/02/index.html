🧱 Anvil Robotics closes a seed round of about 5.5 million dollars to boost its modular platform for building robots.

From the Blog article New robotics and Physical AI course by Javier Martín, first published in Spanish on 8 April 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

🧱 Anvil Robotics closes a seed round of about 5.5 million dollars to boost its modular platform for building robots. The company proposes a kind of "Lego for robots", with open hardware and software kits that make it possible to develop physical AI applications quickly. Its robots can be bought for between 5,000 and 10,000 dollars and ship within a few days. The company seeks to democratize access to advanced robotics for startups, researchers and small teams.
