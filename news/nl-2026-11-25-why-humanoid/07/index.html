🐈 Felis is a cat-inspired quadruped design, with two motors in the articulated spine and one per leg.

From the Blog article Why Humanoid? by Javier Martín, first published in Spanish on 25 November 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

🐈 Felis is a cat-inspired quadruped design, with two motors in the articulated spine and one per leg. In simulation, the robot uses that spine to twist in mid-air and land on its feet. The team also shows how it walks, turns, jumps and dances with six motors.
