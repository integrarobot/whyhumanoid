From the Blog article Why is it important that the Tesla Roadster uses Optimus's actuators? by Javier Martín, first published in Spanish on 21 October 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

🧠 NEURA Robotics presents Neuron AI, a model that integrates 3D vision, touch, force, sound, robot state and language with memory. According to the company, it works on five types of robots in its catalogue and allows skills to be distributed through Neuraverse. In addition, NEURA Robotics partners with NXAI, Sepp Hochreiter's company, to develop robotic memory based on the recurrent xLSTM architecture. They also plan to incorporate an NXAI model into Neuraverse.
