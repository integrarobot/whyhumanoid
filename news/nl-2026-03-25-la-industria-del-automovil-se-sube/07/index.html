Item 7 of 12 from the Blog article The car industry rides the Physical AI wave by Javier Martín, first published in Spanish on 25 March 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

📦 Zalando will deploy up to 50 Nomagic Richard robots in European distribution centres after a pilot with 100,000 picks a day. These robots use computer vision and precision grippers to handle shoeboxes and other products, enabling orders to be processed quickly and supporting employees; the deployment will cover facilities in Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Sweden and France.
