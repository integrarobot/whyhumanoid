🏭 Fagor and Funditec have launched a joint venture to manufacture industrial humanoid robots in Europe.

From the Blog article A method for integrating robots by Javier Martín, first published in Spanish on 1 April 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

🏭 Fagor and Funditec have launched a joint venture to manufacture industrial humanoid robots in Europe. Their first product, Primus P1, will be geared to manipulation and maintenance tasks. The company plans to produce 200 units a year and scale to more than 3,000, combining artificial intelligence with Fagor's engineering experience to facilitate collaboration between robots, operators and industrial machinery.
