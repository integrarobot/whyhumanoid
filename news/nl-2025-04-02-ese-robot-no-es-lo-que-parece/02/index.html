🤖 Returning to the subject of robots, here are two pieces of news about the financial side of the companies developing them: on the one hand, the commercial collaboration agreement between Apptronik and Mercedes-Benz for the Apollo robot to work in the car factories of the prestigious German brand. And on the other, the news of the investment of 23.5 million dollars in the company Dyna Robotics with the aim of deploying AI with a physical body in the real world, with cost-effective, easy-to-deploy robots for companies of all sizes.
