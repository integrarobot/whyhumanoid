Item 9 of 18 from the Blog article What Apple has to do in robotics by Javier Martín, first published in Spanish on 11 November 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

🚨 Ring, Amazon's security brand, creates Ring Robotics with two autonomous devices: Ring Drone Cam, a flying indoor camera, and Ring Rover Cam, a surveillance robot for businesses with a camera on an adjustable mast, video up to 4K and a siren. Both use AI Pilot, a system that creates a map of the space, proposes patrol points and avoids obstacles. The Rover starts shipping in the United States on 16 December for 999.99 dollars and the Drone on 27 January 2027 for 249.99. Amazon warns that the drone does not yet have FCC authorization.
