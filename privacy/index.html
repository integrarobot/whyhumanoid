Privacy

How we handle your information

Why Humanoid? is run by Integrarobot. This page says what we collect, why, and who processes it for us. Questions or requests about your data: javier@liderarmente.com.

Visiting the site
The public pages count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not track individual visitors.
Your account
Your email address, to send sign-in links and to identify your account. Sign-in links work once and expire after 15 minutes. Your browser keeps a sign-in token until you sign out.
Subscription and payments
Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store card details. We keep the subscription state Stripe reports, to open your access.
API keys
We store a fingerprint of each key, never the key itself, with its label and when it was created, last used or revoked; and request counts per key for the fair-use limits.
Custom report questionnaires
Your answers are stored in your account, visible only to you and to the people preparing your report. We use them only to prepare and deliver your report and to answer you about it. They are not published, not used for the public catalogue and not used for marketing.
Emails
Emails about your account and orders are sent through Resend. We do not send marketing emails from this service.
Where data is kept
The app and its database run on Cloudflare. Payment data stays with Stripe.
Your choices
Ask us at javier@liderarmente.com for a copy of your information or to delete it. Deleting an account does not delete payment records that Stripe or we must keep.
