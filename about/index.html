About

About Why Humanoid?

Why Humanoid? is Integrarobot's research catalogue of humanoid robots and the ecosystem around them. It helps people and their software agents explore source-linked evidence and work through business questions about these robots.

What it covers

Humanoids, Deployments, Components, Integrators, Software, Datasets, Benchmarks, Investment, Other robots and Reports.

What a record is

Each record compiles claims made in public sources, with a link to the source. Where the evidence allows, records tell demonstrations, trials and pilots, announcements and operations apart.

A record states what its source says. It is not an independent verification: manufacturer claims are shown as claims, not as verified capabilities, and a missing value stays missing. The Method explains the definitions and how records are kept.

Who makes it

Why Humanoid? is made by Integrarobot, founded by Javier Martín, an entrepreneur and AI educator in Spain. Integrarobot helps companies understand the value of integrating AI-driven robotics.

Using it

The public catalogue is free to browse. Complete Deployments and Components, and custom company reports, are described on Subscription and reports. Software agents can use the same catalogue through the API, the MCP server and the command-line tool: see For agents and the MCP connection's terms of use.
