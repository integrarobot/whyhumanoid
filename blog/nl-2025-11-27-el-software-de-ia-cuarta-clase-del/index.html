AI software. Humanoid Robots Course

VLAs and World Foundation Models are the missing pieces for having fully functional robots working in the real world.

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 27 November 2025 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

Artificial Intelligence software for humanoid robots is one of the great limiting factors at the moment for us to have fully functional machines able to perform most human functions.

But fortunately there are more and more initiatives aimed at meeting this need. Many of them involve tech giants such as NVIDIA and Google. Many startups are also working to solve it.

In this class you will discover the main initiatives for developing vision-language-action models and world foundation models, as well as the earlier software for controlling robots and the sensors that allow them to interact with their environment.
