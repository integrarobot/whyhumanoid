UPS wants to buy thousands of robots

Sales of humanoid robots are starting to accelerate, and in this article we are going to look at some examples.

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 29 April 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

Image: Image

What could lead Brett Adcock to publish a chart on the growth in manufacturing of Figure robots in which the Y-axis data does not appear?

I'm sure he knew this would generate a lot of criticism and even distrust. It doesn't seem the attitude of a businessman who wants to improve the world through his technology.

But I'm not going to be the one to criticize one of the businessmen making the most effort to make the dream of humanoid robots come true, so let's get to what matters: robot manufacturing figures are soaring.

If the chart is true, albeit incomplete, what seems clear is that in recent months manufacturing has soared, going from 0 or 1 robot in April 2025 to a number that should be several hundred robots manufactured per month at the moment, which would take us to a figure of thousands over the course of this year.

So, if we refer to the figures the company publishes, we have the figure for the manufacturing capacity the BotQ factory has or will have: "*with capacity to produce up to 12,000 humanoid robots a year*".

📦 UPS wants to buy 10,000 robots from Figure

This is where things get interesting, because in such a new activity, with so much uncertainty, the normal thing would be to be cautious about launching into mass production, lest the market not yet accept the product because of its immature state of development.

But you know, nothing ventured, nothing gained, and the reward is so high that many companies are rushing to buy robots, even though they are still at a premature stage of development. Remember what I always say: the first iPhone had no GPS, but people rushed to buy it en masse.

Let's look at other examples of how companies are rushing to buy, or at least order, humanoid robots.

  • Schaeffler plans to deploy more than 1,000 Hexagon AEON robotic humanoids in its global factories by 2032
  • Operators of China's power grid plan to buy thousands of robots in 2026 alone, including devices to inspect and maintain vital infrastructure.
  • ROBOTERA, which is deploying its L7 humanoid robot in more than 10 logistics centres operated by China Post, SF Express Group and other companies in the sector.

It's moving!

My conclusion after thinking about all this is that things are advancing at their proper pace. So we have time to prepare and not have everything come crashing down on us as happened with Generative AI, where many people are still paralyzed because they don't know how to face the great change taking place.
