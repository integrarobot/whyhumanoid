A community to bring together robotics enthusiasts

There is so much going on in Generative AI that robotics may be overshadowed. Pioneers who want to drive Physical AI are needed.

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 28 January 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

Image: Image

I admit that right now I'd like to be tinkering with Moltbot (formerly Clawdbot), but here I am, writing this week's Integrarobot newsletter. And it's not a complaint; I simply understand that the temptation with everything happening in Generative AI is so great that robots can often take a back seat.

But fortunately I'm clear about my goals, and if you're reading this it's because you also glimpse the great opportunity of robotics.

The opportunity of Physical AI could be even greater than that of Generative AI, but since there are still technical problems to solve, here we are still talking about expectations instead of working on real things.

But this should start to change. We have to join forces to get robotics projects off the ground.

That's why I'm launching the Robot Pioneers Community. I've shortened the name to simplify it and not scare people with something as futuristic as humanoids. And I'm convinced that there is a lot that can already be done to bring the future we dream of closer to us.

Let's start by recording the operations carried out in our companies, whether it's a factory, a shop, a workshop, a gym, … There are thousands of places where robots will be able to help, but we need to give them data. For example, yesterday the new version of the AI from Figure was presented, the company that is best communicating its advances in humanoid robotics, and they tell us this:

*Helix 02 has been trained with more than 1,000 hours of human motion data and sim-to-real reinforcement learning. Thanks to this, the single neural system is able to control the robot's entire body directly from images, enabling agile, long-horizon autonomy.*

Another thing that can be done is to set up a robot rental company. I know that many tech events want to start covering this topic, but of course they don't want just videos and photos; they'd like to have a real robot walking around. Since the most accessible one right now is Unitree's G1, and it is also becoming famous on TV thanks to the work Synergy Tech is doing, it is surely a good option for a while until there is more variety on the market.

But anyway, these are just a couple of ideas about what we could start doing, beyond some people wanting to devote themselves directly to developing hardware and software for robots; in the previous newsletter I also described all the possibilities I see for entrepreneurship and investment in the Physical AI sector.

So what I wanted to do today is encourage you to take part in the community, with which we will hold a first online meeting next week, specifically on Wednesday 4 February at 19:00. Will you join us?
