Is GPT-6 Astra the ChatGPT moment of robotics?

The presentation of the new version of ChatGPT is going to become a new boost for the development of robotics.

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 11 September 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

Throughout this year there has been a lot of talk about the ChatGPT moment of robotics, and until now the dominant opinion was that there would be no moment equivalent to what happened on 22 November 2022, when ChatGPT was presented to the public; rather, the arrival of intelligent robots would be a more gradual process, because small-scale milestones kept occurring one after another that, taken together, could constitute a phenomenon similar to what happened with Generative AI, but in the field of Physical AI.

But the presentation of GPT-6 Astra on 3 September 2026 could become a milestone of such magnitude that it ends up becoming the reference moment for the take-off of Physical AI.

What happens is that many things had to happen to get here: just as in Generative AI we have to remember milestones such as AlexNet and Transformers, in Physical AI we must also be aware of all the work that has been done on developing VLAs and WAMs by startups such as Skild AI, Generalist, Field AI, Physical Intelligence, Genesis AI, OpenMind, … as well as by the robot manufacturers themselves (Tesla, Figure, 1X, …) and major technology companies such as Google, NVIDIA, Meta, Xiaomi, Alibaba, …

So what has just happened is that the big AI labs, specifically OpenAI and Anthropic, have just joined this race to develop AI for robots — well, DeepSeek too, from the moment it became an investor in Unitree before its IPO.

And the result of adding a new type of competitor to this race will be a new acceleration, bringing us ever faster toward the dream of general-purpose robots.
