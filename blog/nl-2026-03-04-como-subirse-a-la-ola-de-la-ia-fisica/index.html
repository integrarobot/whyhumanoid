How to ride the Physical AI wave

On 25 March I am organizing a webinar to set out the opportunities that robotics driven by artificial intelligence is going to offer.

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 4 March 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

When I come across news like the creation of the company Noble Machines by engineers from Apple, SpaceX, NASA, DeepMind, Amazon and Tesla, who are leaving their jobs to found a company creating a new humanoid robot, I can't think of anything other than helping more and more people who, like these brave souls, want to ride the Physical AI wave.

It will be risky, no doubt, but it will be worth it.

Right now I could be recording a video or writing an article about the latest thing I've learned about Claude or the latest thing I've done with Lovable, but here I am, convening an event to talk about the opportunities Physical AI is going to offer.

Image: Image

How to ride the Physical AI wave will be an online event I am going to hold on 25 March from 19:00 to 20:00, where I will share the professional and business opportunities that general-purpose robotics is going to offer and what the first steps should be to become part of it.

At this event you will discover the opportunities emerging to work, found companies and invest with the development of Physical AI and general-purpose robotics.

I'm Javier Martín, founder of Integrarobot, and I devote myself to spreading the word about the great opportunity before us with Physical AI, so that any professional or company can ride this great wave, which is going to be even bigger than that of Generative AI.

In this webinar you will be able to learn:

  • What Physical AI is, what general-purpose robots are and what state of development they are in at the moment.
  • What we can learn from the most relevant initiatives being carried out in this sector.
  • Professional and business opportunities that are going to arise in the AI-driven robotics sector.
  • First steps to take in this field, both professionally and in companies.
  • Without forgetting the synergies that arise with other types of AI robots such as autonomous vehicles, drones, ...

The time has come to take action; let's not let the fireworks of Generative AI keep us from seeing the enormous opportunity Physical AI represents. It won't be as simple as asking a chat to do your homework; we'll have to get our hands dirty. It's going to be hard, but it will be worth it.

When the social media boom began, Peter Thiel said "*we wanted flying cars, instead we got 140 characters*"; let's not let the same thing happen to us with Physical AI: "*we wanted humanoid robots, and we got chatbots*"

We really need AI to reach the physical world; there are so many problems to solve: people who suffer physical injuries from jobs that harm them, elderly people who have no company to help them with routine tasks, many people who come home after a long, hard working day and have to keep working to keep their house in order, countless companies that cannot grow for lack of staff, … I don't think I need to go on: society and the economy have reached a point where we need this technology, and we have to do everything possible for it to reach the market as soon as possible.

Hardware and software manufacturers are doing the most important part, but now it's the rest of us's turn. There are a thousand things to do, starting with outreach, which is what I'm focusing on with Integrarobot, but training, advisory services, robot training, deployment plans in companies, sales, rental, repair, app creation, … and many other things that will emerge as robots reach the market will be needed.

It's time to take action; we have to ride the Physical AI wave. See you on 25 March at the online event. And if you set out to found a company in the robotics sector, I hope you can share it with me so I can help you spread the word.
