In 2 years Optimus could begin transforming the world

Elon Musk expects the Tesla Optimus robot to start having a significant impact by 2029.

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 16 February 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

We already know that Elon Musk's estimates are never fulfilled on the when, but they are on the what and the how. So after letting the genie out of the lamp for electric and autonomous vehicles and for reusable rockets, it seems the next big play should be humanoid robots.

Because Optimus is not just a robot; it is Tesla's definitive bet on automation. But everything indicates that repeating the self-driving car play is not going to be so easy. First, because robots can do a thousand things, whereas cars do only one, and to add complexity to the matter, cars drive on roads with very well-defined rules, but robots will have to be exposed to millions of different situations, one for each owner.

According to ARK Invest's research, humanoid robots are 200,000 times more complex to develop than robotaxis. Given Elon's first-principles approach and his tenacious determination, we project that Tesla's Optimus will begin to transform life in factories, and then life at home, in 2028/2029. Cathie Wood

We begin this new weekly series of articles at Integrarobot with what could become the quintessential humanoid robot, although Optimus will not find it easy to compete with Figure, 1X's Neo, Apptronik's Apolo, Boston's Atlas, … its main competitive advantage is having Tesla's manufacturing experience and commercial know-how. So the competition is going to be enormous, especially if we also count everything that may come from China.

But anyway, let's get to the point and look below at what we know about Optimus and what manufacturing expectations we have in the short term.

---

Tesla Optimus technical sheet

Optimus is the humanoid robot developed by Tesla to automate physical work in human environments, starting with its own factories. It integrates bipedal locomotion, advanced manipulation and an AI system based on Tesla's autonomous driving stack.

The strategy is to integrate hardware, software and manufacturing to scale to mass production and reduce costs below 20,000 dollars once high manufacturing volume is reached.

Applications

  • Internal automation in Tesla factories
  • Handling and sorting of parts
  • Data collection for training physical AI models
  • Research in imitation learning and end-to-end control
  • Future generalist household tasks

Key aspects

  • Bipedal humanoid platform with its own actuators
  • Reuse of the FSD stack for perception and navigation
  • End-to-end neural architecture
  • Advanced hand with 22 degrees of freedom
  • Massive training infrastructure through Dojo and GPU clusters
  • Internal deployment strategy before commercialization

Main specifications

  • Approximate height: 173 cm
  • Estimated weight: 55–60 kg
  • Actuators: 28 in Gen 2
  • Gen 3 hand degrees of freedom: 22 DoF
  • Payload: not officially published
  • Autonomy: not publicly specified

Construction

  • Lightweight structure optimized for weight reduction
  • Battery integrated in the torso
  • Design geared to manufacturing at scale

Computing

  • Proprietary hardware derived from FSD
  • Training in Tesla data centres and Dojo

Sensors

  • Vision-based camera system
  • Force and torque sensors in joints
  • Tactile sensors in the hand
  • Internal IMU

Actuation

  • Its own linear and rotary actuators
  • Integrated reducers
  • Precision control through AI

Power

  • Proprietary electrical system
  • Integrated battery
  • Autonomy not published

Software

  • End-to-end neural networks
  • Imitation learning
  • Teleoperation for data generation
  • Simulation and massive training

Safety

  • Human supervision in the pilot phase
  • Hierarchical control architecture
  • Initial use in controlled industrial environments

Production and economics

  • 2025 target: thousands of units
  • 2026 target: tens of thousands
  • Long-term target: millions
  • Mass production not yet started

Cost

  • Target manufacturing cost: < 20,000 USD
  • Estimated price: 20,000–30,000 USD
