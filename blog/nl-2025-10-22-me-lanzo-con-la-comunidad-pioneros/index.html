The Pioneros Robots Humanoides community is born

The moment of truth has arrived: I want to bring together the people who want Humanoid Robots to become a reality soon.

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 22 October 2025 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

The time has come to step forward and launch the Pioneros Robots Humanoides community. After 10 months devoted to in-depth study of Physical AI technology, or robotics driven by artificial intelligence, the moment of truth has arrived.

The Pioneros Robots Humanoides community is the new stage on this path I am travelling, with the aim of spreading the word about the opportunity this technology will offer, both for professionals and for companies that want to surf the next big wave of Artificial Intelligence.

Who are the Humanoid Robot Pioneers?

I have been on the Internet for 30 years and creating digital businesses for 25, but I have always had the feeling of arriving late to the sector that has done so much to shape today's world. I say this because I was not a user of the first computers, the legendary Spectrum, Amstrad, Commodore, while many of my friends and colleagues were, so I always felt a bit self-conscious about not having touched technology so early and so closely as to later become a developer.

I don't want the same thing to happen to me with robots, and what I want is to help others, the more the better, to take advantage of this great opportunity. Those are the people I call pioneers: those who are not afraid to venture into the wild west of robotics, because it is true that the technology is not yet as mature as I would like, but on the other hand what I have seen this year is that the speed at which it is evolving advises against getting distracted, because if you're not careful you'll miss the best of the party.

Isn't it too early for Humanoid Robots?

Yes, it may be too early, I don't want to deceive anyone, but I feel compelled to launch as soon as possible, and I am willing to persevere as long as it takes until the day many predict arrives: the ChatGPT moment of robotics.

When will that moment be when robots fulfil their promise? That is what everyone asks me, and my answer varies between "two or three years at most" and "sooner than you imagine", which is usually followed by many more doubts from the people I'm talking to.

But the important thing is not knowing whether it is too early, but what we can do now, and then everyone can decide how to position themselves. So for now the first thing to do is learn, because we are not talking about buying a household appliance; this technology is much more complex and will require making many decisions along the way to buying your first robot.

This is the reason why the first thing I have decided to do after the newsletter is launch a course, because I believe that before offering other services such as consulting or even selling robots, there is still a lot to learn and research. So in the course what I will do is share what I have learned this year, and those who sign up for the course will have all that knowledge concentrated in a few hours of valuable content.

So what is the Pioneros Robots Humanoides community about?

The course is really an excuse to start making things happen. We could even say it is like a material reward for taking part in a crowdfunding campaign to fund the launch of the Community.

Because what really matters here is the Community: bringing people together so that things happen. That is what I have done most of the time I have been an entrepreneur: Loogic, Iniciador, Smart Money, Futurizable, and now with Integrarobot.

The Pioneros Robots Humanoides Community will be the meeting point for those who want to become protagonists of this new technological revolution. And I'm not just talking about building robots or software for robots. Because there is much more that can be done in this new technological ecosystem that is beginning to take shape.

But anyway, let's get to the point, because I'm starting to ramble. What I am looking for with the community is to bring together (virtually for now) business owners, executives, professionals, technologists of all kinds, entrepreneurs, and even students, to generate opportunities. Some because they can integrate the technology to maximize productivity in their companies, others because they want to develop new business ideas, also those looking for job opportunities; in short, everyone who wants to take advantage of what will be the next great technological revolution after Artificial Intelligence.

What do you offer me and what do you ask of me for being part of the community?

As I said, the first thing I am going to do is a course, which will be recorded on video and can also be taken live online. I commit to having the recorded course available at the beginning of December, and it will last about 10 hours. And the live online course will have a first edition on 16, 17 and 18 December from 18:00 to 20:00.

In addition to this, so that being part of the community means learning a lot about humanoid robots, I am going to produce a PDF guide on the state of the art of this technology, and once I have run the first course I will start holding monthly online community meetings and weekly video updates to complement the content of the newsletter and the course.

What I want, therefore, is for the Humanoid Robot Pioneers to be the best informed and prepared about everything that is happening with robotics driven by Artificial Intelligence, the opportunities this will generate and how to take advantage of them.

You've convinced me. How do I join the Community?

It's simple: you have to upgrade your subscription. If you are already subscribed to this newsletter for free, you can join the Pioneros Robots Humanoides Community by paying an annual fee of 100 euros, which will give you access to the recorded course, the live online course, the state-of-the-art guide and the weekly video updates. Oh, and also to everything else I set up, because this is just the beginning 🙂

If I haven't convinced you yet, go to this link, where I tell you everything in more detail and you can see the syllabus of the Humanoid Robots course.

That's all, my friend; know that I am willing to do whatever it takes to make this community succeed. I admit I'm afraid because of the uncertainty, but I'm here for the long term and I'm going to do what I can to push it forward. I don't want Spain and Latam to be left out of the great technological boom of Humanoid Robots.
