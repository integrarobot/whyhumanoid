Vision and Language - Humanoid Robots Course

Vision and language are the way the robot connects with

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 4 December 2025 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

[Video embedded in the original article]

Vision and language are two of the two fundamental pillars that allow humanoid robots to function in real environments with a level of intelligence and naturalness that until now seemed unattainable.

Thanks to vision systems, capable of interpreting images, depth and the geometry of the environment, robots can recognize objects, understand scenes, follow movements and navigate safely through dynamic spaces.

At the same time, language models allow them to understand verbal commands, interpret complex instructions and hold fluent conversations with people.

When both worlds are combined in vision-language models, the robot gains a much richer contextual understanding: it can associate words with elements of the environment, reason about what it sees and plan coherent actions to carry out tasks.

This integration turns humanoids into agents capable of perceiving, interpreting and acting adaptively, closing the loop between perception, reasoning and movement through Language-Vision-Action models, which are defining the new generation of robots driven by artificial intelligence.
