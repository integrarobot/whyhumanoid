Why Humanoid?

Let me introduce my new project within Integrarobot

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 25 November 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

Image: Image

Today I'll tell you what I've been working on over the last week, because I think it may significantly change the work I have done over the last two years with Integrarobot.

Until now everything I've done has been outreach about the humanoid opportunity. I have also tried to create the community of humanoid robot pioneers, but I've had to pause it until I find a better way to scale, which I suppose will depend a lot on having a larger audience so that a small part of it decides to get more involved in the project. So, since I had been wanting for some time to create a more scalable service, I've launched Why Humanoid?, which I hope to turn into the world's reference source of information on humanoid robots.

Yes, it sounds ambitious, and I assure you that when I started with Integrarobot I couldn't have imagined creating something like this, but now, thanks to the power of AI Agents, I've realized that the only limit is my imagination.

Because what you can currently see if you visit Why Humanoid? is the result of 5 days of work. Admittedly, fed by the two years I've spent compiling information about this Physical AI sector.

So what I want to do today is tell you my plans for this project, through which I hope to contribute everything in my power so that more and more professionals and companies can take advantage of the great opportunity humanoid robots are going to offer in the coming decades.

  1. The first thing is what you see now: a kind of database with a lot of information about robots and their deployments in industry, as well as components, software, datasets, benchmarks, integrators, investments, … In short, everything or almost everything that currently makes up the humanoid robot industry.
  2. The second is who this is aimed at, and well, here I have to explain my vision of the future, because the target audience for this content is not people but Agents, since I believe that soon they will be the ones we ask to help us make decisions related to robots, and that is where they will need the knowledge I am generating here.
  3. The third is what you can't see: the business model. And this is where the experience of two years working with Integrarobot is bringing me the most value, because what has been coming my way has been, on the one hand, companies that want to integrate robots and, on the other, industries that want to diversify their business by working in this sector. And this is what I offer through personalized reports.
  4. And the fourth is what I hope will be the most important business in the long term: access to the data, especially for the Agents entrusted with the job of creating a robot integration or industrial diversification plan. Because these Agents could look for the info by themselves, but this would cost much more than a subscription to all the content I offer. So if those Agents are really intelligent, I hope they choose Why Humanoid? ;)

That said, let me tell you how I'm building this project, and I do so because I would love you to give me your opinion and tell me how I could improve it.

  1. I'm building it all with ChatGPT (Codex) and Claude (Code). The former coordinates and the latter executes. The two understand each other quite well, and it's incredible to see them work autonomously. Well, I've had to contribute a bit, because what I've done is create a folder on my computer with Karpathy's LLM Wiki to serve as the memory for the whole project and thus make all the agents' work easier. Also, if I run out of credits I sometimes turn to Grok Bot or Gemini to help me generate more content that the others then add to the database.
  2. The content is currently in a Google spreadsheet, which is what I started this robotics sector data collection project with. And the application is hosted on Github. I don't know whether all this is the best option when we come to scale, but it's what ChatGPT and Claude decided between them. So for now I'm relaxed, because I know that if we need to migrate to any other infrastructure they'll be able to do it without problems. Some developers surely have a different opinion here, but in my case that's what comes of being an AI fanatic.
  3. Not everything always goes well; that's what comes of working with something so new, but in the end things get fixed. As long as the token budget allows it, of course, because this week I've been on this project is the first time it has systematically exhausted all the tokens of all the AIs I subscribe to; in fact, I've had to move up to higher tiers, so I'd better start billing with this or I'll go broke :)

And this is the story of how I'm building whyhumanoid.com. What else can I tell you? Oh yes, it would be great if you shared it with your friends. Thank you very much, and see you next week.
