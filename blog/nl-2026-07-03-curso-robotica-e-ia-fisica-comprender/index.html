Robotics and Physical AI Course - Understanding the current state of development of robots

The transition from waiter robots to humanoid robots

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 3 July 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

In this new video of the Robotics and Physical AI course, exclusive to members of the Robot Pioneers Community, we share our view of the current state of development of AI robotics.

To do so, we use the example of waiter robots as a prelude to the arrival of humanoid robots, and better still, a type of mobile robot equipped with robotic arms that increasingly resembles what humanoids will end up being.

As a reminder, the three phases on which we are focusing this new course are:

PHASE 1. Understand: learn about the current state of development of physical AI and how it represents the transition from programmed robotics to new machines capable of perceiving, deciding and acting in the real world.

PHASE 2. Apply: learn to translate everything happening in the robotics sector into real integration opportunities, identifying tasks, sectors, maturity criteria and conditions for deploying robots in companies, services, the home, ...

PHASE 3. Prototype: imagine and represent through digital tools how the next robotic systems are programmed, trained and work, connecting sensors, models, decisions, actions, human supervision and business models.
