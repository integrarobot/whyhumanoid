Current challenges - Humanoid Robots Course

Below we analyze the main challenges facing robotics and Physical AI during 2026.

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 27 February 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

If in 2025 the buzzword in robotics was vision, language and action models, this year we keep hearing about world foundation models. This gives us to understand that robotics driven by Artificial Intelligence still faces technological challenges on which its future depends.

But in addition to these challenges on the technology side, specifically the software side, we must also look at two other large blocks: the economic and the social.

Will we manage to build robots so cheap that people can buy them en masse? If robot manufacturers manage to build them for a price somewhere between that of a household appliance and that of a car, I predict humanoid robots will sell by the millions.

Will society accept the general-purpose robot model, or will it refuse to let them into the home? It will all depend on the usefulness offered and on how well designed they are to minimize the possible risks that may arise when they interact with people.
