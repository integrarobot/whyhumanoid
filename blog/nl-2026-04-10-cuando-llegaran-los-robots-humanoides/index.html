When will robots arrive? - Humanoid Robots Course

Neither late nor early: it is time to prepare and seize the opportunity.

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 10 April 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

When people ask me when humanoid robots will arrive, I would like to give them a specific date, like the presentation of the iPhone on 9 January 2007 or 30 November 2022 for the presentation of ChatGPT. But in this case it is not going to be so simple, because the complexity of the technology is much greater and the uncertainty at this moment is still enormous.

What can be said, and it is what most of the studies we have read show, is that adoption is going to happen in stages.

First, robots are arriving in industrial environments. Factories, warehouses and logistics centres where tasks are repetitive and the environment is fairly controlled. It is the most logical place to start, because the economic return is easier to measure and the risks are lower.

Then will come expansion into services. Hotels, hospitals, building maintenance, urban logistics. Environments where there is already more interaction with people and where the robot has to face somewhat more variable situations.

And finally, when the technology matures and costs fall enough, we will see much more generalist humanoid robots. Robots capable of working in many different types of environments, from construction to agriculture or even homes.

This is not just my personal opinion, but a pattern that appears quite consistently in the reports of consultancies and investment banks analyzing this sector.
