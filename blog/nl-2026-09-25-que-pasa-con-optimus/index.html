What's going on with Optimus?

This year Tesla has said very little about new developments in the Optimus humanoid robot

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 25 September 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

Tesla's silence about the state of development of the Optimus humanoid robot raises many doubts, and in this video from the Integrarobot course I give my opinion about what may be happening.

The recent start of mass production of the Tesla Semi truck can give us quite a few clues about what may be happening with Optimus. Basically, no factory, no robot. And if Tesla's plan was to manufacture 1 million robots a year, it first had to dismantle the factory where the Model S was produced, and then move on to manufacturing the robot.

So I think that in this case the silence is not so bad. While the company makes the excuse that it doesn't want competitors to copy it, Tesla's share price remains fairly flat, and that gives us the opportunity to keep accumulating for when the market wakes up to this company's true potential.
