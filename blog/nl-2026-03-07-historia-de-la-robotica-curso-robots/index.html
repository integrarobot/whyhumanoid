History of robotics - Humanoid Robots Course

What the history of robotics teaches us about the arrival of humanoid robots.

By Javier Martín · First published in Spanish on 7 March 2026 · English translation dated 27 November 2026 · Original edition

The history of robotics can give us some clues about how this technology may develop in the coming years.

First of all, it is worth highlighting the need humanity has had throughout history to create machines that solve specific physical needs, starting with clocks and even automata that used common components.

Later, as soon as computing began to develop, some scientists immediately set to work on developing robots, even using what can be considered one of the first attempts at artificial intelligence, especially in computer vision.

After those first attempts at building robots came the AI winter and, apparently, the robotics winter too, so it can be seen that this technology has also been through hard times. Will they happen again? There may be ups and downs, but I don't think it will stall as much as it did in past decades.

In any case, what the history of robotics conveys to us is the existence of a solid foundation on which everything we are now experiencing is built, and how that foundation should serve to give this technology the solidity it needs to succeed.
