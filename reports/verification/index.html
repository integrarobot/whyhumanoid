Verification coverage baseline: November 2026

Initial primary-source review of 25 models across payload, energy runtime, task autonomy and industrial maturity, with additional version and customer/document cross-checks for selected models. No physical testing, performance replication or complete operational audit was performed.

25 primary-source screenings; 0 completed deep audits. This is the first dated baseline, not a completed quarter or a claim that the selected robots have been independently tested.

Source preservation: response copies captured for 38 of 45 source URLs in this cohort; 0 have a public archive URL. The per-source preservation records below show capture dates and SHA-256 hashes. These are preservation copies, not performance audits.

Unassigned HRL and zero confirmed HRL4+ mean insufficient evidence reviewed under these gates; they do not establish that no such deployments exist. This editorial cohort is not a statistical sample, and its counts cannot be generalized to the sector.

Selection: Editorial cohort spanning North America, Europe and Asia; industrial products, household platforms and research robots; bipedal and wheeled configurations; mixed commercial and research visibility. This is not an objective world top-25 ranking or a representative statistical sample.

  • A source review is not an independent audit.
  • Not established means absent or unresolved in the inspected sources, not absent everywhere.
  • A product-page claim remains V1 even when the page calls a section Technical Specifications.
  • Existing catalogue values are preserved; this overlay does not silently replace the data source.
  • HRL assessments cover only their named source-backed activity. No HRL4+, rated-load duty cycle or independent physical audit is claimed.

A quarterly comparison requires another dated evidence review; this launch baseline does not claim a completed quarter.
