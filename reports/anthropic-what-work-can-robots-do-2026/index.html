What work can robots do?

Builds a task-level index of which US work tasks robots could perform, scoring each task with Claude and cited robot sources, and compares job exposure with historical wage and employment data (section "Measuring exposure"). It covers robots in general, defined as autonomous physical machines that sense and act, not only humanoid robots.
