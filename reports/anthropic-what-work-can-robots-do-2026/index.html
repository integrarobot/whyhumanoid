Report · Anthropic · 30 September 2026

What work can robots do?

Builds a task-level index of which US work tasks robots could perform, scoring about 19,000 O*NET tasks with Claude and cited robot sources, and compares job exposure with historical wage and employment data and with robot costs. Covers robots in general, not only humanoids.

Checked against the original Robots can already do most physical work in some setting but are much more expensive than human labour; automation will first reach jobs where robots already have a foothold.
