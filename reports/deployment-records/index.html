How to read it

Counts are of records, not of robots or sites. Every group with fewer than 3 records is added to «Other», so no number points at a single record. The year is the year the record was observed or, if that is missing, published. How records are collected and labelled is in the method; who and where, record by record, is on each company and robot page; the records in full are in the Deployments subscription (US$100 per year).
