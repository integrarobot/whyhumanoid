Report · Binh Pham (flower and bolt) · November 2026

What robotics companies look like, and what they’re betting on

An independent study of 652 robot companies in 44 countries: their robots, brands and words, the money behind them (912 funding rounds of 411 venture-backed companies) and the AI models they release. Covers robots of all kinds, with humanoids and robot AI as one group.

Checked against the original Robot money moved to humanoids and robot AI; most of China's humanoid unicorns deliver while most of the West's do not; China now publishes and opens most robot AI models.
