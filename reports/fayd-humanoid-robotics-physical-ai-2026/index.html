US$5.83 to US$8.75 per productive hour

Fayd's model: a US$100,000 robot working 6,000 productive hours a year costs about US$5.83 per productive hour; a US$50,000 robot working 2,000 hours, about US$8.75. Both exclude financing, integration and supervision.
