The Robot Frontier Is Accelerating

A research post built on the publisher's own Robot Intelligence Index (25 benchmarks of robot policies, in simulation and on real robots, as of 23 November 2026): how fast the leading policies improve, how many tasks they solve, at what time and cost, and two conditional projections to the end of 2027. The ratings are the publisher's own and not independently verified.

Checked against the original Frontier robot capability on the publisher's index is accelerating: relative capabilities rose more than 9x in three months.
