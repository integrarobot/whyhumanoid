Costs and economics · Estimate of a current value Checked against the original

about US$55,000 per unit

NEXERY itemises about US$43,000 of hardware and software inside a selling price of about US$55,000; the rest is R&D costs and margins, not itemised.

  • Motors and transmissionUS$18,900
  • SensorsUS$7,400
  • Precision servomotors for degrees of freedomUS$5,500
  • Control and learning software (AI)US$3,900
  • IT hardware and chipsUS$2,700
  • Navigation (camera, LIDAR, radar)US$2,700
  • Body partsUS$1,000
  • Battery and self-chargingUS$900

The eight items add up to the stated ~$43,000 hardware-and-software subtotal; R&D and margins make up the rest of the ~$55,000 price and are not itemised.
