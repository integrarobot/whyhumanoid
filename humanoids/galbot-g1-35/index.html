G1 by Galbot

G1 is a humanoid robot by Galbot (China). Platform: Wheeled humanoid on 4-wheel omnidirectional chassis (600×600 mm); 650 mm leg-lift travel; max 1.5 m/s; IP54. Recorded height 173 cm, weight 92.5 kg, payload 5 kg and runtime 8 h.
