GoMate by GAC Group

GoMate is a humanoid robot by GAC Group (China). Platform: Full-size wheel-legged humanoid; variable 4-wheel / 2-wheel mobility, climbs stairs. Recorded runtime 6 h.

Before comparing: Height depends on mode; weight not in GAC release; first deployment on GAC car lines (inspection)
