H2 by Unitree

H2 is a humanoid robot by Unitree (China). Platform: Full-size bipedal humanoid with bionic head with facial features. Recorded weight ~70 kg and runtime ~3 h. Price and availability: USD 29,900 for H2 (tax and shipping excluded, manufacturer page accessed 2026-11-19); H2 EDU: contact sales. Warranty 8 months (H2) / 12 months (EDU).

Before comparing: Rated 7 kg vs peak 15 kg arm payload: use rated value for tasks. Base H2 does not support secondary development. Leg joint 360 N·m is peak torque
