Home robot (model name not disclosed) by PokeBot

Home robot (model name not disclosed) is a humanoid robot by PokeBot (China (Beijing)). Platform: Wheeled base with two arms, according to founder interview (QQ News, 2026-09-03); company rejects bipedal humanoid form..

Before comparing: Pre-commercial: demos and benchmarks are company-controlled. Team ~20 people (2026-04). Founder Xu Huazhe: Tsinghua IIIS assistant professor, TEA Lab, ex-co-founder and chief scientist of Galaxea (left 2026-02). Galaxea is also an investor: check technology/IP overlap. Beijing entity subject to Chinese data and cybersecurity law
