CLOiD by LG

CLOiD is a humanoid robot by LG (South Korea). Platform: Head, torso with two arms, wheeled base with autonomous navigation; tilting torso adjusts height; no legs. Price and availability: Price and launch date not disclosed; LG to pursue domestic pilot safety certification with KIRIA (MOU signed 2026-11-13, no timeline).

Before comparing: LG's CES releases call it a home robot; LG lists it among its humanoid robots in the KIRIA release (2026-11-16). Do not carry over KAPEX specifications. LG does not say CLOiD uses AXIUM actuators.
