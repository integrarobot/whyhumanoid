Next-Gen IRON by Xpeng

Next-Gen IRON is a humanoid robot by Xpeng (China). Platform: Bipedal humanoid with humanoid spine, bionic muscles and full-body flexible skin; body-shape customization.

Before comparing: First-gen IRON (2024) was 178 cm / 70 kg; XPENG has not published Next-Gen IRON height/weight (press citing 178/70 refers to gen 1). Some press quotes 2,250 TOPS vs official 3,000 TOPS effective
