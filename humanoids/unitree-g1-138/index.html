G1 by Unitree

G1 is a humanoid robot by Unitree (China). Platform: Bipedal humanoid; foldable (690×450×300 mm folded). Recorded height 132 cm and runtime ~2 h. Price and availability: USD 13.5K for G1 base (tax and shipping excluded, manufacturer page accessed 2026-11-19); G1 EDU: contact sales. Warranty 8 months (G1) / 18 months (EDU).

Before comparing: Base G1 does not support secondary development; arm load varies greatly with posture
