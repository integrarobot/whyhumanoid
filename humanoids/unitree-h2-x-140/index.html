H2-X by Unitree

H2-X is a humanoid robot by Unitree (China). Platform: Full-size bipedal humanoid; positioned for security patrol, industrial, commercial and home services. Recorded height 172.5 cm. Price and availability: USD 25,400 for H2-X (tax and shipping excluded, manufacturer page accessed 2026-11-19); H2-X EDU: contact sales. Warranty 8 / 12 months.

Before comparing: Security-patrol positioning: check intended use and local rules. Weight varies by configuration. Do not transfer H2 values
