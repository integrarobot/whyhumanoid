R1 by Unitree

R1 is a humanoid robot by Unitree (China). Platform: Lightweight bipedal humanoid. Recorded height 123 cm and runtime ~1 h. Price and availability: From USD 4,900 (R1 AIR); USD 5,900 (R1); R1 EDU: contact sales (tax and shipping excluded, manufacturer page accessed 2026-11-19). Warranty 6 / 8 / 12 months.

Before comparing: Arm figure is listed by Unitree under arm joint torque but given in kg; confirm before relying on it. R1 AIR has fewer DoF and a monocular camera
