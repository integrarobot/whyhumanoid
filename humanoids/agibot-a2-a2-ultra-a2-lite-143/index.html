A2 (A2 Ultra / A2 Lite) by Agibot

A2 (A2 Ultra / A2 Lite) is a humanoid robot by Agibot (China). Platform: Full-size bipedal humanoid for reception, guidance and performances. Recorded height 169 cm. Price and availability: Price not disclosed (contact sales).

Before comparing: Company claims >1,000 units deployed, a 106.286 km Guinness walk and CR/CE-MD/CE-RED/FCC certification: verify certificates. Lite lacks perception sensors
