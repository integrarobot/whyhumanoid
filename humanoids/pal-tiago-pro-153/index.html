TIAGo Pro by PAL

TIAGo Pro is a humanoid robot by PAL (Spain). Platform: Omnidirectional mobile manipulator: 4 mecanum wheels, lifting torso (35 cm range), pan-tilt head; PAL calls it a mobile manipulator, not a humanoid. Price and availability: Price not disclosed.

Before comparing: Reach: ~92-96 cm vertical, 236-240 cm horizontal depending on configuration. Borderline humanoid form
