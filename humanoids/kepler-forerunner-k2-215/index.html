Forerunner K2 by Kepler

Forerunner K2 is a humanoid robot by Kepler (China). Platform: Bipedal or wheeled; Bipedal humanoid (Basic / Bipedal Development versions); Wheeled Development version on intelligent wheeled chassis (135 kg). Recorded height 175 cm, weight 75 kg and runtime 8 h. Price and availability: USD 30,000 base model (company release, 2025); delivery lead time 90 days; warranty 8 months (Basic) / 12 months (Development versions).

Before comparing: 75 kg applies to bipedal versions (wheeled version 135 kg); DoF varies by version (30 vs 52); price from 2025 release, confirm current quote
