Gallop by ASUS

Gallop is a humanoid robot by ASUS (Taiwan). Platform: Fixed base with two arms today; wheeled version announced as next. Price and availability: Price not disclosed; Computex 2027 showcase; customer pilots 2027; availability targeted 2H 2028.

Before comparing: Training and testing at an ASUS group EMS station, not production. 99.5% within 20 s is a target, not a result. Factory and customers not disclosed. Social links are ASUS corporate accounts, not the robot's.
