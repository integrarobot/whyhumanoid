DR02 by Deep

DR02 is a humanoid robot by Deep (China). Platform: Industrial all-weather bipedal humanoid; IP66; -20 to 55 °C; stairs 25 cm, slopes 20°. Recorded height 180 cm. Price and availability: Price not disclosed ("place an order").

Before comparing: 4 m/s is an extreme lab figure; operating max 1 m/s. Base DR02 has only 4-DoF arms
