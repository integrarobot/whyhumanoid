Origin by HIRO Industries

Origin is a humanoid robot by HIRO Industries (USA (Corvallis, Oregon)). Platform: Stationary dual-arm platform for human-scale workstations, with a 1-DoF lift; no legs and no mobile base stated. Not a bipedal humanoid. Price and availability: Manufacturer-stated total price USD 9,950 for the complete dual-arm robot. Reservation = USD 1,450 deposit, fully refundable any time before shipping (request by email); USD 8,500 balance due only when HIRO confirms the build is ready to ship. Estimated delivery Spring 2027, US addresses only; if it moves, buyer may cancel for a full refund (hiroindustries.com/reserve, accessed 2026-11-19). Taxes, shipping and warranty not stated.

Before comparing: Pre-delivery: the deposit buys a queue place, not a delivered unit. Kitting and light-assembly examples are labelled "Illustrative" by the company. Team of 2 (YC page). Location discrepancy: reserve page says Corvallis, Oregon; YC page says San Francisco. CEO Lex Roller was a founding engineer at Nori Robotics (row: Nori)
