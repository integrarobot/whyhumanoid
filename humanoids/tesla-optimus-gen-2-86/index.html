Optimus Gen 2 by Tesla

Optimus Gen 2 is a humanoid robot by Tesla (USA). Platform: Bipedal general-purpose humanoid; stairs and uneven terrain (Oct 2024); articulated toes and foot force/torque sensors; 30% faster walk vs prior gen (Dec 2023); obstacle/person avoidance via onboard nets; max speed ~8 km/h (2021 design target). Recorded height 173 cm and weight 57 kg.

Platform and mobility
Bipedal general-purpose humanoid; stairs and uneven terrain (Oct 2024); articulated toes and foot force/torque sensors; 30% faster walk vs prior gen (Dec 2023); obstacle/person avoidance via onboard nets; max speed ~8 km/h (2021 design target)
Degrees of freedom and grippers
Neck 2 DoF (Dec 2023). Gen 2 hands (Dec 2023): 11 DoF, tactile sensing on all fingers. Later Optimus hand (Musk Jan 2025; patent Apr 2026): 22 DoF biomimetic, tendon-driven, actuators in the forearm, 25 actuators per arm (50 for both hands). Whole-body DoF not published.
Sensors and compute
Eight cameras and the same Autopilot computer as Tesla vehicles (2021 announcement); joint position encoders; force/torque sensors in the feet; tactile sensing in all fingers; actuators with integrated electronics (Dec 2023); rear camera used to dock at the charger
Battery and charging
Tesla-designed battery pack (AI Day 2022). Can locate the nearest charging station and dock autonomously using only the rear camera (Oct 2024). Capacity, voltage and runtime not published.
Software
Tesla end-to-end neural nets on the vehicle Autopilot/FSD computer; onboard real-time inference; training from human demonstrations; multi-robot shared environment understanding

Autonomy and supervision
Autonomous general-purpose humanoid (balance, navigation, perception). End-to-end onboard nets; autonomous error correction; learns tasks from human demonstrations; can fetch and hand over items on request. Tested in Tesla factories and offices (Nov 2025). Still in development; not a finished consumer product.

Market

Price and availability
No list price. Tesla/Musk targets: production cost <$20,000 at >1 million units/year (Jan 2025); long-term cost $20,000–$30,000 (Oct 2024). Selling price to be set by demand. Pilot line running at Fremont (Nov 2025). Internal Tesla use first; start of deliveries to outside companies estimated 2H 2026 (Jan 2025, rough). Gen 3 production line described as larger and due in 2026.
