# Blog and News: ingest contract and procedure

Two public English editorial sections of whyhumanoid.com:

- **Blog** (`/blog/`): every Integrarobot newsletter article published in 2025 and 2026, main body only. The section
  was first prepared as Newsletter; its `/newsletter/` addresses are kept as noindex redirects to the matching `/blog/`
  address. Edition ids keep their `nl-` prefix and the source publication is still the Integrarobot newsletter.
  The More news block is not part of it.
- **News** (`/news/`): every item of the More news block, one page each, in the order its edition gave it, linked to
  that edition. It can also publish standalone Integrarobot roundups that Javier supplies for News, with the
  publication credited directly and the sources linked on each item.

They are independent of the catalogue:
- they have no workbook tab, no import into D1, no subscription gate and no Worker code;
- they are not part of the Wednesday 14:00 catalogue update.

Codex runs the editorial feed separately as the heartbeat `feed-newsletter-and-news`, every Wednesday at 16:00
Europe/Madrid. Its first scheduled date is 2026-12-02. The working specification is `wiki/proyecto/newsletter-y-news.md` in
the local Why Humanoid workspace.

The company headlines on listed company pages (`assets/news.js`, `GET /v1/news`) are a different feature and stay as
they are.

## Where things live

| Path | What | Published |
|---|---|---|
| `editorial/config.json` | `published` switch (false until the publication change), `embargo_until`, `scope.years` | no (excluded in `_config.yml`) |
| `editorial/inventory.json` | every edition the source listing shows, its status, the listing pages read, completeness | no |
| `editorial/editions/<edition_id>.json` | one edition in English, with its More news items and translation record | no (source of the pages) |
| `editorial/originals/<edition_id>.json` | optional Spanish original of that edition, kept as evidence | never |
| `editorial/roundups/<roundup_id>.json` | a reviewed standalone Integrarobot roundup, independent of the newsletter inventory | no (source of the News pages) |
| `editorial/state.json` | content hashes written by ingestion | no |
| `blog/`, `news/` | pages generated by `node tools/build-pages.mjs` | yes, live since 2026-11-27 |
| `newsletter/` | redirects of the former addresses to `/blog/`, generated by the same build | noindex |

Code:
- `tools/editorial-model.mjs`: validation, readiness and coverage;
- `tools/editorial.mjs`: the command line;
- `tools/editorial-view.mjs`: the pages and the safe Markdown renderer;
- `tools/test-editorial.mjs`: tests, with synthetic fixtures in `tools/fixtures/editorial/` only.

## Inputs

For a standalone roundup, add one file under `editorial/roundups/` with an ID of the form
`roundup-YYYY-MM-DD-slug`. Record the title, publication, publication date, English language and named review
with evidence. Give each item its position, headline and English Markdown with at least one web source. Keep
published positions stable because the item URLs depend on them. The newsletter ingestion leaves these files
in place. Review with `node tools/editorial.mjs check` and regenerate with `node tools/build-pages.mjs`.

The local extraction session delivers two English Markdown documents, which are not in Git:

- `articulos-integrarobot-desde-2025.md`: the articles (main bodies);
- `mas-noticias-integrarobot-desde-2025.md`: the More news items.

They are expected under `outputs/` of the local Codex Integrarobot workspace. Do not assume they are complete because
they exist: completeness is a property of the inventory (below), not of the files.

The source is delivered in English, so ingestion does not translate again. Spanish originals are optional evidence:
- when available, include them, and the edition is then checked against them (same links, same items, unchanged
  hash);
- when not, the edition is accepted on its own.

Codex converts the two documents into one **package** (JSON, contract below) and imports it with
`tools/editorial.mjs`. The package itself stays outside Git; `state.json` records its SHA-256.

## Package contract (version 1)

`node tools/editorial.mjs template` prints an empty package. Every field below is required unless marked optional.
Unknown fields in an edition are refused.

```text
package_version     1
source.publication  "Integrarobot"
source.listing_url  the archive or listing page the inventory was read from
source.retrieved_on YYYY-MM-DD
source.files        [{ name, sha256 }] for each Markdown document converted
source.pagination   { pages: [every listing page URL read, in order], exhausted: true|false, note }
complete            false, unless the inventory has been reconciled with the listing read to its last page
completeness        null, or { declared_by (agent or person), declared_on (YYYY-MM-DD, not before
                    source.retrieved_on), evidence (how the inventory was reconciled with the listing) }
inventory[]         every edition the listing shows, ingested or not:
  edition_id        nl-YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>: the original publication date and a short lowercase slug,
                    at most 90 characters; stable forever once ingested
  source_url        canonical https URL of the original edition (one edition per URL)
  published_on      YYYY-MM-DD, original publication date, in 2025 or 2026, equal to the date in edition_id
  status            ingested | pending | inaccessible
  note              required for inaccessible (why it could not be read); else null
editions[]          the editions this package imports (may be only the new ones):
  edition_id, source_url, published_on   as in the inventory, whose status must be ingested
  retrieved_on      YYYY-MM-DD the source was read (not before published_on)
  source_language   "es"
  language          "en"
  author            byline as published
  title             English title
  subtitle          English subtitle or null
  body_markdown     English main body in Markdown, WITHOUT the More news block
  more_news         { status: present | absent | inaccessible, note, items[] }
    status present      the edition has the block; items lists every item, none skipped or merged
    status absent       the edition has no such block; items is []
    status inaccessible the block exists but could not be read; items is []; note says why
    items[]             { position: 1, 2, 3... in source order, headline (English), body_markdown (English, may be "") }
  translation       { translated_on, translated_by, status: draft | reviewed, reviewed_by, reviewed_on, review_evidence }
    translated_on   date the English text was produced (not before published_on); distinct from published_on
    status reviewed after a substantive review of the English against the source by an identified reviewer:
                    Codex or Claude (the authorised agents) or a person. Validation alone is not a review.
                    reviewed_by names the reviewer, reviewed_on dates it, review_evidence says what was compared
                    and found (for example: "title, body and 7 items compared with the Spanish edition; no omissions")
  original_sha256   null (computed by ingestion when an original is supplied)
  original          optional, null or the Spanish source:
                    { language: "es", title, body_markdown (without the block), more_news { status, note, items[] } }
```

### Converting the two documents

1. Inventory first. Read every page of the source listing until there is no next page and record each URL in
   `source.pagination.pages`. Set `exhausted: true` only if the last page was reached. Every edition found goes in
   `inventory`, including those not converted yet (`pending`) or unreadable (`inaccessible`, with a note).
2. One edition per original URL. Pair an article with its More news items by the edition's canonical URL and date,
   never by title.
3. Main body: the article as written, without the More news heading and everything under it.
   - Keep the headings (`##`, `###`), paragraphs, lists, quotes and links as Markdown.
   - Do not use HTML. Images become Markdown images, which are shown as links.
4. More news: one item per entry, in the order of the edition, numbered from 1.
   - The same headline in two editions stays two items: an item is identified by `<edition_id>/<NN>`.
   - Keep every link of the item: the source of a news item is its attribution.
5. Links: the English body and items keep exactly the URLs of the original. When the original is supplied, ingestion
   refuses any difference.
   - Accepted targets: http(s) URLs, and `mailto:` with the author's business address only (`server/src/support.js`),
     as published in the article. Any other mailto, `tel:` or other scheme is refused.
   - Google documents (Docs, Sheets, Drive, Forms) are refused: the site never publishes links or identifiers of
     the master workbook or of Drive (AGENTS.md). An article that linked one needs a reviewed substitution, applied
     to the English edition and to its archived original alike, before ingestion.
6. Dates: `published_on` is the original publication date. `translation.translated_on` is when the English text was
   produced, and the pages show both. Never use the retrieval or translation date as publication date.
7. Mark `translation.status` as `reviewed` only after a substantive review of the English against the source, by
   Codex, Claude or a person, recorded in `reviewed_by`, `reviewed_on` and `review_evidence`. Passing
   `tools/editorial.mjs check` is validation, not a review. Draft editions are imported but not published.
   Javier does not approve each edition by hand.

## Procedure

```bash
node tools/editorial.mjs ingest --package <package.json> --dry-run
```
```bash
node tools/editorial.mjs ingest --package <package.json>
```
```bash
node tools/editorial.mjs check && node tools/editorial.mjs report
```
```bash
node tools/build-pages.mjs && node --no-warnings --test tools/test-editorial.mjs
```

Then a branch, a PR against `main`, green CI and Codex review, as for any change.

Ingestion:
- validates the whole store as it would be afterwards and writes nothing if anything is wrong;
- is idempotent: the same package twice writes nothing the second time;
- is incremental: a package may carry only new editions, and earlier ones are kept;
- never removes an ingested edition, and refuses an inventory that demotes one;
- changes an edition already reviewed only with `--accept-change <edition_id>`.

News item identity:
- A News item's address is `/news/<edition_id>/<NN>/`: its edition and its position.
- What sits at each position is identified by its links, or by its headline when it has none. A change of count,
  of order (even with the same count) or of which story sits at a position is an identity change.
- Ingestion refuses an identity change unless `--accept-item-change <edition_id>` is passed. Use it only to correct
  a wrong mapping before the edition is published.
- Once an edition is live on the site, an identity change is refused outright: an existing item URL must keep
  showing the same story. Retiring or redirecting a published item is a separate reviewed code change.
- A wording fix in an item's text keeps its identity.

`report` gives, per year:
- editions listed, ingested, pending and inaccessible;
- More news present, absent and inaccessible;
- News items;
- translations reviewed and draft;
- Spanish originals archived;
- what is ready to publish.

It says "Coverage is not declared complete" unless the inventory declares completeness, which requires:
- `pagination.exhausted` and the listing pages actually read (`pagination.pages`, https URLs, no repeats);
- `source.listing_url`, `source.retrieved_on` and `source.files` (each converted document with its SHA-256, kept as
  provenance in `inventory.json`);
- at least one edition and none pending;
- `completeness.declared_by`, `declared_on` (not before the listing was read) and `evidence`.

## A new year

`editorial/config.json` `scope.years` lists the years the store accepts, consecutive from 2025 (default
`[2025, 2026]`). An edition from a year outside it is refused, so the weekly feed must not run into January unnoticed.
Before the first edition of a new year:
1. add the year in a small reviewed change, for example `[2025, 2026, 2027]`;
2. rebuild the pages, which adds the year archive pages.
Then the feed continues as before.

## Publication

Pages are generated on every build. The sections are live only when `published` is true **and** today, in
Europe/Madrid, is on or after `embargo_until` (2026-11-27). `tools/editorial.mjs check` also refuses
`published: true` before that date. Until then (the state on this branch):
- they carry `noindex`;
- they are not in the sitemap;
- no page links to them;
- with no ready edition they say that nothing is published yet.

Only editions with a reviewed translation are rendered. Spanish originals never are.

Publication (authorised by Javier on 2026-11-27, done in PR 177):
1. `published` is true, live from `embargo_until` 2026-11-27 (Europe/Madrid);
2. Blog and News lead the shared footer (`tools/footer-view.mjs`: home, generated pages and app) and the home page's
   Also line; the ten categories of the header are unchanged;
3. rebuild and verify the pages in a browser.

The sitemap entries follow automatically.

## Display contract (Javier, 27 Nov 2026)

Fixed in `tools/editorial-view.mjs` and `assets/editorial.css`; `tools/test-editorial.mjs` fails if it regresses.
The weekly feed changes data only and never this display.

- **Blog article** (`/blog/<edition_id>/`): the main body alone, with title, subtitle, dates and the link to the
  original. No generated "News in this edition" section, list, count or note about the roundup, whatever
  `more_news.status` says. Blog cards carry no news count.
- **News index and year archives** (`/news/`, `/news/<year>/`): grouped by month and edition, in source order. Each
  item shows its whole `body_markdown` once, through the safe renderer, with its own source links (the company or
  source words link to the source URL, never to our item page).
  - No heading generated from the first sentence, no excerpt, no truncation, no numbered list.
  - The only internal link is a small separate "Permalink" to `/news/<edition_id>/<NN>/`, which does not repeat the
    text. The paragraph is never wrapped in an internal link.
  - An item with an empty body shows its headline as its text.
  - The search indexes the whole text of each item.
- **News item page** (`/news/<edition_id>/<NN>/`): a neutral heading "News, <date of the edition>", the metadata
  (item N of M, parent Blog article, dates, original edition), the whole body once with its source links, and
  previous and next. The stored `headline` may title the page (`<title>`), never the visible heading.
- **No images** (Javier, 27 Nov 2026), in the Blog or News: no picture, image Markdown, alt text, caption or fallback
  link such as "Image: Image", and no empty paragraph, list entry, quote or heading left where one was. The renderer
  drops them (`withoutImages`); the store and the archived originals keep them unchanged. Prose around an image stays,
  and so do ordinary links, even to an image file, when they have their own label.
- Bold and links in the body are kept as written. The text is never edited in the HTML: a wrong text is corrected in
  the store, through the reviewed ingest.
- Addresses never change: 121 Blog articles, 780 News item pages, the year archives and the `/newsletter/` aliases.

Never part of this stream:
- workbook or Sheets schema changes;
- subscription, payments or the catalogue;
- synthetic or sample records in `editorial/`. A test fails if fixture text reaches the store or the pages.
