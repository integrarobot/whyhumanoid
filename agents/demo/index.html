Why Humanoid? in ChatGPT

A real ChatGPT session using the read-only Why Humanoid? connection, recorded on 26 November 2026: three questions and the answers ChatGPT gave with the catalogue's tools.

What it shows

  1. Logistics deployments. A question about humanoid robots in logistics. The answer cites three public catalogue entries with their source links and keeps announcements, pilots and reported operation apart.
  2. The Robot Learning Data Atlas. What the atlas covers and why dataset sizes cannot simply be compared.
  3. A request to change a record. Asked to change the deployment status of GXO, ChatGPT declines: the connection only reads, and none of its tools can write.

How it was recorded

  • A real ChatGPT session with real answers, not a mock-up.
  • Captured with Claude in Chrome, which takes one frame per action: 49 real frames in three segments, kept in their full order and each held long enough to read. It is not continuous video, and the timing does not measure how fast anything responds.
  • The connection was selected and each question typed before capture began, so the account's personal add-ons do not appear.
  • The answers reflect the catalogue at the time of recording, not live counts.

Use it yourself

The same read-only tools are available to any MCP client, and to the HTTP API and command-line tool: see Agents. Using the connection is subject to its terms of use.
