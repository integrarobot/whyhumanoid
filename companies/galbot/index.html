Robots

  • G1G1 is a humanoid robot by Galbot (China). Platform: Wheeled humanoid on 4-wheel omnidirectional chassis (600×600 mm); 650 mm leg-lift travel; max 1.5 m/s; IP54. Recorded height 173 cm, weight 92.5 kg, payload 5 kg and runtime 8 h.
  • S1S1 is a humanoid robot by Galbot (China). Platform: Wheeled dual-arm robot on 4-wheel-steer omnidirectional chassis (730×730 mm); 750 mm lift; no legs. Recorded weight 320 kg and runtime 8 h. Price and availability: Price not disclosed.
  • ET1ET1 is a humanoid robot by Galbot (China). Platform: Bipedal humanoid for entertainment and performance. Recorded height 125 cm and weight 32 kg. Price and availability: Price not disclosed; Galbot news (2026-09-03) says pre-orders open.
