Subscription and custom reports

One subscription. The full catalogue, for you and your agents.

Most of Why Humanoid? is free. The data subscription opens the detail behind every deployment case and every component, for you on the web and for your software agents. A custom report is something else: our analysis applied to one decision of your company.

What the subscription includes

Price
US$100 per year, billed annually. No free trial. Taxes are not added at checkout: the charge is the price shown.
Deployments
All 125 cases in detail: where the robot works, the commercial relationship where documented, the task and workflow, integration, the timeline and every source claim, reported results with their conditions, how the evidence connects, confidence and open questions.
Components
The full technical passport of all 19 products, with the source of every value, side-by-side comparison and technical filters.
Everything else
The other categories stay free for everyone, in full.
What it is not
Research and data, not a personalised recommendation. Missing information stays missing. Custom reports are not included.

Four ways to use it

The same subscription and the same account work everywhere. There is no extra charge for agents.

On the web
The app: browse, search and compare, with every field.
HTTP API
Your agents call the same API as the app, with an API key from your account. Setup
MCP
An MCP server for AI assistants and agent frameworks that support remote MCP servers. Setup
Command line
A single-file tool for scripts and terminals (Node.js). Setup

Fair use: 60 requests a minute and 5,000 a day per API key. Up to five active keys per account; revoke any of them at any time.

Renewal and cancellation

The subscription renews each year at its own price until you cancel. Cancel at any time from your account, in the Stripe billing portal, and keep access until the end of the paid year. Subscriptions bought earlier keep their contract, currency and renewal price.
