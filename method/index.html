Method and sources

How Why Humanoid? records robots and deployments

Why Humanoid? records what named sources say about humanoid robots, the companies that make them and the organisations that test or use them. Every value is shown as recorded, with its source; a record is a claim, not a verified fact.

Robots

The catalogue holds 134 humanoid robots with their specifications, company and market fields, links and the date their sources were last checked. All of it is free. A value the sources do not give is shown as not published or not disclosed, never estimated.

Deployment records

A deployment record is one claim from one source about an organisation, a robot and a task. One deployment can have several records: an announcement, a pilot, a later report. Each record keeps who made the claim and when, its status as recorded, a verification label and an evidence level.

In operation
The source says the robot is working in operation.
Pilot or trial
The source describes a pilot, trial or test.
Announced
Planned or agreed, not evidence of work.
Unconfirmed
Claimed by a vendor, or unresolved.

Verification labels say who reported the claim, as recorded: customer confirmed, partner reported, vendor reported, media reported or unresolved. The evidence level is a code from E0 to E5, shown as recorded; its definitions are not published.

Selected evidence

Each robot and company page shows one of its records with its site, task, status, metric, evidence level, verification label and source. The record is chosen by a fixed rule among those with a source link and a verification other than unresolved: the most advanced status as recorded (in operation, then pilot or trial, then a vendor's claim of operation, then announced), then a primary source (customer, maker or partner) before the press, then customer confirmation, then the latest publication, then a record with a metric. The claim text, notes and the other records stay with the Deployments subscription.

Robots, companies and records

A record is linked to a catalogue robot only when it names that model or its model line, never because it names the maker alone. When it names the model line, the page says the record may concern another version. Companies are identified by the organisation id of the sheet or by the maker's name; no corporate data is added.

Updates

The database is updated every week from the Integrarobot newsletter, checked against primary sources. Each page shows the snapshot date of its data.

Visits

The public pages count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not track individual visitors.

What is public and what is for subscribers

Public
Humanoids and every category except Deployments and Components, in full: Humanoids, Integrators, Software, Datasets, Benchmarks, Investment, Other Robots; a page for every robot, company and other robot; counts and summaries of deployment records (organisation, robot as recorded, country, sector, status and year); one selected record per robot and company; the first 10 rows of Deployments and Components; this report and method.
Subscription
Complete Deployments and complete Components (Why Humanoid? Deployments and Components). Every deployment record in full: site, task, metrics, claim text, verification, evidence level, sources and notes; search with tags over every category; relations between robots, companies and records (€100 per year).

Public counts never reveal a single record: the report groups anything under 3 records.
